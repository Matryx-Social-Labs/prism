Russia sanctions bill advances in US House, set for final vote

The US House of Representatives advanced the Lindsey O Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, which authorizes tariffs on nations trading oil and gas with Russia.

MITIHBHTHI5 outlets · 15 reports · English
Follow storyShare

The record Reader

Written from the 15 reports below. Nothing here is unsourced.

The US House of Representatives has passed the Lindsey O Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, authorizing the president to impose tariffs of up to 100% on countries buying significant volumes of Russian oil and gas, with an amendment explicitly naming India and other major buyers. What began as a sanctions push against Moscow's energy trade now threatens steep US duties on goods from India and China, the largest buyers of discounted Russian crude. Indian business coverage, ranging from neutral reporting to alarmist takes, frames the bill as a serious trade risk for India. Delhi has responded by saying it will protect its economic interests, Beijing rejects the measure as unilateral long-arm jurisdiction, and the Kremlin has criticized the bill. Whether the Senate passes it and how the president applies the tariff authority remain unresolved. Watch Indian and Chinese crude-buying patterns and any signs of trade retaliation.
