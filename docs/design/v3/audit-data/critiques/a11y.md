## 4. Constraints for the new design system

1. Targets ≥44×44 px, ≥8 px apart, at 360×640.
2. Text ≥4.5:1 (large 3:1), both themes, pixel-sampled over photos and blur.
3. No text under 12 px.
4. Every state passes in a greyscale screenshot, both themes, and has a word.
5. State lines, control borders ≥3:1.
6. Pinned chrome ≤15% of 360×640; none at 200% zoom.
7. Line height ≥1.6 Indic, ≥1.8 Urdu; zero clipped glyphs in 13 scripts.
8. Italics on Latin only.
9. Urdu blocks start-aligned, arrows mirrored.
10. Reduced motion (untestable from stills): zero moving transitions, no auto-advance.

## 5. What already works

1. One source: dashed border plus "one source so far".
2. Verified badge, photo pills and links hold in phone dark; dark text measures clean.
3. Indian-language quote upright, dashed rule, "reported"; italics Latin only.
4. Selected states use inversion, bold, underline; inputs show a focus ring.
5. Counts printed beside every bar.
